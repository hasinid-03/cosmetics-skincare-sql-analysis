# 🧴 Cosmetics & Skincare Sales Analysis — SQL Project

## About
Analysed cosmetics & skincare sales data spanning
6 countries, 10 salespersons and 15 products
using SQL in MySQL Workbench.

## Dataset Columns
salesperson, country, product, sale_date, amount, boxes_shipped

## Questions Answered
1. Which sales happened in India?
2. Which UK sales had Cream in the product name?
3. Which country made the highest total sales?
4. Which product has the best average order value?
5. Which salesperson made more than 10 sales?
6. Which sales were above average amount?
7. Each sale compared to overall average with performance label
8. Salespersons above average total revenue (CTE)
9. Rank all salespersons by total revenue
10. Running total of revenue over time

## Key Findings
- USA had the highest total sales of $628,488 among all 6 countries
- Olivia D'Souza ranked #1 salesperson with $387,406 total revenue
- 6 out of 10 salespersons performed above average revenue

## SQL Concepts Used
SELECT, WHERE, AND, LIKE, GROUP BY, HAVING,
Subquery (WHERE & SELECT), CASE WHEN,
CTE (WITH clause), Window Functions (RANK, Running Total)

## Tool Used
MySQL Workbench
